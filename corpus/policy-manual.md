# Calder County Social Services Policy Manual
**Effective Date:** 31 December 2025  
**Document Version:** 4.2  

---

## Part 1: General Administration and Scope

### §1.1.1 Authority and Purpose
This manual sets forth the operational policies and eligibility standards governing benefit distribution across Calder County Social Services programs. All caseworkers and administrative staff are required to apply these rules consistently across all applicant assessments.

### §1.2.3 Jurisdiction
The policies contained herein apply exclusively to residents of Calder County. Referrals for residents of non-adjacent counties must be directed to the State Department of Human Services central routing office.

---

## Part 2: Income and Financial Eligibility Criteria

### §2.1.1 Gross Income Definition
Gross monthly income includes all earned income, unearned income, disability benefits, and pensions received by household members prior to tax deductions.

### §2.2.4 Income Verification Standard
Applicants must supply official documentation (such as pay stubs for the preceding 60 days, tax returns, or bank statements) to verify claimed gross monthly income.

---

## Part 3: Assistance Programs and Benefit Calculation

### §3.1.2 General Assistance Calculation
Monthly benefit amounts are calculated as the difference between the standard maximum allowance for the household size and 50% of the household's countable monthly net income.

---

## Part 4: Recipient Reporting Obligations

### §4.3.2 Deadline for Reporting Changes in Circumstance
Recipients must report any change in household income, employment status, or residence address to the county agency within 10 business days of the change occurring. Failure to report within 10 business days may result in temporary suspension of benefits or overpayment recoupment.

---

## Part 5: Dependent and Specialized Care Allowances

### §5.4 Care Allowances and Dependent Support
Childcare and adult dependent care allowances are available to recipients who are actively employed or participating in an approved vocational training program. The maximum monthly allowance is $450 per qualifying dependent upon submission of certified care receipts.

---

## Part 6: Housing and Emergency Relief Grants

### §6.2.1 Emergency Shelter Assistance
Emergency housing grants are available once per 12-month period to households facing imminent eviction or homelessness, subject to fund availability.

---

## Part 7: Special Populations and Student Eligibility

### §7.1.3 Full-Time Higher Education Students
Students enrolled full-time in accredited higher education institutions are generally excluded from standard general assistance, unless they satisfy the specific student exemption criteria set forth in §5.4.

---

## Part 8: Employment and Training Requirements

### §8.1.1 Work Registration Mandatory Requirement
Able-bodied adults without dependents must register with the County Employment Office and complete at least 20 hours per week of qualifying work activities or job search preparation.

---

## Part 9: Program Compliance and Administrative Reviews

### §9.1.4 Schedule for Reporting Household Modifications
All changes in household composition, marital status, or income alterations must be reported by the primary beneficiary within 30 calendar days of the event. Failure to report within 30 calendar days may trigger a compliance review.

---

## Part 10: Overpayment Recoupment and Penalties

### §10.2.1 Recoupment Rates
Overpayments resulting from administrative error or late reporting will be recouped by deducting up to 10% of the recipient's monthly benefit disbursement until the balance is satisfied.

---

## Part 11: Appeals and Fair Hearings

### §11.2.1 Right to Appeal
Any applicant or recipient dissatisfied with a benefit decision, reduction, or termination has the right to file a written appeal with the Calder County Fair Hearing Board within 90 days of notification.

---

## Part 12: Supervisory Escalations and Guidance

### §12.0.1 Unresolved Manual Conflicts and Supervisory Referrals
In cases where policy interpretation is ambiguous or manual provisions appear contradictory, caseworkers must not make unilateral determinations. The case must be formally escalated in writing to the Senior Policy Supervisor for written ruling.
